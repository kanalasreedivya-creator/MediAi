Know What To Do

Emergency Tips

Important steps to take during medical emergencies

1

Stay Calm

Take a deep breath and stay calm. Panic can make the situation worse. Assess the situation carefully before taking action.

2

Call Emergency Services

Dial the emergency number immediately. Provide clear information about the location, the nature of the emergency, and the patient's condition.

3

Provide First Aid

If you're trained, provide basic first aid. Stop bleeding, perform CPR if needed, and keep the patient warm and comfortable while waiting for help.

4

Stay with the Patient

Stay with the patient until help arrives. Keep them calm, monitor their breathing and consciousness, and provide reassurance.

5

Have Information Ready

Keep important medical information handy: allergies, medications, medical conditions, and emergency contacts.

6

Follow Instructions

Listen to the emergency dispatcher and follow their instructions. They are trained to guide you through emergency situations.
